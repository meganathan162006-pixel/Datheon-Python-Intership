Strategic retention recommendations

  1. Prioritize month-to-month customers: observed churn is 42.7%, compared with 2.8% for two-year contracts. Offer transparent annual-plan incentives and early renewal outreach.
  2. Investigate electronic-check churn: this group has 45.3% churn. Offer easier automatic payment options and monitor onboarding friction.
  3. Review fiber-optic experience: observed churn is 41.9%. Examine service reliability, support wait times, installation quality and price-value perception.
  4. Proactively support new customers: tenure under 12 months shows weaker retention in month-to-month contracts. Use 30/60/90-day onboarding and service check-ins.
  5. Improve support adoption: compare churn among customers with and without TechSupport and OnlineSecurity; test targeted support bundles and measure outcomes.
Data quality: 7,043 customer records; 11 blank TotalCharges values retained as missing (not imputed); no duplicate customer IDs. Cohort caveat: the supplied file has tenure but no customer start date or observation date. Therefore this matrix is an observed retention-rate segmentation by current tenure band and contract, not a true time-based acquisition cohort survival curve. Churn associations are descriptive, not causal.
